## Why these fifteen

Heroic-arc heavy by design — that's what I watch — but deliberately includes
breakers: Whiplash for ambiguous-victory endings, La La Land for
yearning/bittersweet, Ragnarok for comedic-dominant register, [Bollywood
choice] for non-Western narrative shape, Into the Spider-Verse for coming-of-age.

## Known v1 gaps

- Pure horror / existential dread is undertested — flagged for v1.x corpus expansion.
- No documentary or strict realism — same.
